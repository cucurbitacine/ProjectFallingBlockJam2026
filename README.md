# Make it long, Chef

## About
Made during [Falling Block Jam 2026](https://itch.io/jam/falling-block-jam-2026).

You are a master chef who has decided to make the longest spaghetti on the planet. Build your tower, dodge dangers, watch your step, and stretch your spaghetti all the way up to the stars!

##### Chef controls
* [A] [D] - Move left, right
* [Space] - Jump

##### Figure controls
* [←] [→] - Move left, right
* [↑] - Rotate
* [↓] - Drop 

##### Health points, timer & length
* Play the game and you'll got it

## Project

When you think of falling blocks, the first thing that probably comes to mind is Tetris. But I decided to turn the classic formula upside down: instead of trying to stop the tower from growing, you have to build it as high as possible.

The game jam's additional theme, "Spaghetti," helped me give the tower a purpose: you're building it to make your spaghetti as long as possible. The theme also inspired me to create the main character, Chef.

The game was made in Unity. All sprites were created by myself in Aseprite. Sound effects and music were taken from free sources, including [sfxr.me](https://sfxr.me/) and [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com/music/). Some icons were taken from a free asset pack from the [Unity Asset Store](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/2d/gui/icons/gamepad-and-keyboard-input-sprites-for-textmesh-pro-310114).
